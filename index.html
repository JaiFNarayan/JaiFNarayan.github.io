About me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Rice University (Class of 2029) who likes building at the line where hardware meets software: firmware, PCBs, digital logic, and robots that can see.

Right now I write ESP32 firmware and design custom PCBs for AR/VR haptic systems in Prof. Robert LiKamWa's Experimental Pixels Lab, help characterize a high-frequency synthetic aperture radar in the Everitt Lab, and design RF telemetry hardware for Rice Eclipse's high-altitude rockets. Before Rice, I co-founded and co-captained my school's FIRST Tech Challenge team and took it to the World Championship.

What I work on

  • Embedded systems

    ESP32 and Raspberry Pi firmware, Linux kernel drivers, sensors and closed-loop motor control.

  • PCB & RF design

    KiCAD boards from 2-layer motor drivers to a 4-layer ~900 MHz telemetry board, validated on the bench.

  • Digital logic & FPGA

    Verilog RTL, including a single-cycle 16-bit processor targeting a Xilinx Spartan-7.

  • Robotics & vision

    OpenCV lane tracking, YOLO object detection, PD control and competition robotics.

    Sep 2025 — Present
